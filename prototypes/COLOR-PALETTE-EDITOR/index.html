Color Palette Editor

Revised palettes

Every bar shows its palette color on the left half and the light color the Poll Card draws on the right half, with sample text that crosses both. The palettes start from the copies in palette-data.js; the light color and the text colors are derived by the real functions in src/public/pollCard.js, which this page loads. Under each bar are its background color, the text color on the background and the text color on the fill, with their contrast ratios. Press a row's Change color button to pick a new color; its hex and name update. Names come from the nearest standard color name and can be edited.
