Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-01 · Last updated 22 October 2026

In short

  • We set one cookie to keep you signed in, a second one for 15 minutes while a sign-in link is waiting, and a third for 5 minutes while you sign in with Google, Apple, Microsoft or Amazon. Nothing else.
  • We use your email address to sign you in, to send you the emails you ask for, and to show who voted on polls you take part in. Never for marketing.
  • No tracking: no analytics, no advertising, no profiling, nothing that follows you to other sites.
  • We never sell your data or share it for anyone else's purposes.
  • You can delete your account at any time from your Profile page.

1. Who we are

CrowdPoll (crowdpoll.vote) is operated by CrowdPoll, 6 Spruce Street, Cambridge, Ontario, Canada, N1R 4J9. For anything about your privacy, write to privacy@crowdpoll.vote. For other questions, write to support@crowdpoll.vote.

2. What we collect and why

  • Your email address: to create your account, to sign you in by emailing you a one-time link, to send the other emails you ask for (a change of address, a deleted account), and to identify you on the polls you create, are invited to or vote in. It is never shown to other users. The creator of a poll you were invited to, and CrowdPoll's administrators, can see it.
  • Your Google, Apple, Microsoft or Amazon account: if you sign in with one of them, it tells us the name and email address you choose to share, and we use them as above. We keep no picture, contacts or anything else from your account there. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we store the relay address Apple gives us as your email address.
  • Your user name and avatar: shown with your polls and, on polls that do not hide voters, with your votes.
  • Your polls and votes: the polls you create, and the choices you make on polls, with the time.
  • Addresses other people give us: a poll's creator may type your address to invite you to a private poll, or to bar you from one. We use it only for that poll. If you have no account and want it removed, write to us.
  • Your IP address: used for a few minutes to limit how many emails or requests one connection can make, which stops abuse. It is not stored with your account or your session.
  • Mail you send us: kept to answer you.

Apart from the name a sign-in provider shares, we do not ask for, and do not want, your name, age, location, payment details or anything else.

Invitations to a private poll: when a poll's creator invites you to an Invitation Only poll, we email you the invitation at their request, and at most one reminder if they re-send it. We use your address only for that poll. The invitation's button can sign you in to CrowdPoll once, for up to 30 days or until the poll ends; after that, or once it has been used, it signs nobody in. If the creator removes you after you were emailed, the poll keeps your address, in a normalized form, only so that adding you back does not email you again; it goes when the poll is deleted. If you have no account and want your address removed from a poll, write to privacy@crowdpoll.vote; it is removed with the poll in any case.

3. Why we are allowed to

We use your data to provide the service you asked for when you created your account, and to keep CrowdPoll secure. We do not rely on consent for anything, because we do nothing that would need it.

8. Your rights

  • Delete your account: on your Profile page, under Your Data. This deletes your account, your sessions, your bookmarks and the polls you created with their votes. Your votes on other people's polls stay counted but no longer carry your address or name; they show as "Deleted user". If you were barred from a poll, that record stays, so deleting an account cannot be used to get around it. If you changed your email address, anything recorded under your earlier address is removed when you ask us at privacy@crowdpoll.vote.
  • See your data: write to privacy@crowdpoll.vote and we will send you a copy within 30 days.
  • Correct your data: change your user name or email address on your Profile page, or write to us.
  • Complain: if you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or to the data protection authority where you live.

9. Children

CrowdPoll accounts are for people 16 or older.

10. Poll update emails, later

We may later offer emails telling you when a poll you follow changes. They will be off unless you turn them on, each will have a one-click way to stop them, and we will update this policy before they start.

11. Changes to this policy

The version and date at the top change whenever this policy does. If we make a change that matters, we will email every account 30 days before it takes effect.
