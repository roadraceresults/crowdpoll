In short

  • One cookie lasts: the one that keeps you logged in. Two temporary cookies exist only during login: one for 15 minutes while a login link is waiting, and one for 5 minutes while you log in with Google, Apple, Microsoft or Amazon. Nothing else.
  • We use your email address to sign you in, to send you the emails you ask for, and to show who voted on polls you take part in. Never for marketing.
  • No analytics, no advertising, nothing that follows you to other sites. We do record a fingerprint of your device and browser when you log in and vote, to help uphold one account per person.
  • Before you log in, a quick check on our own servers detects bots and AI agents. It sets no cookie.
  • We never sell your data or share it for anyone else's purposes.
  • You can delete your account at any time from your Profile page.

2. What we collect and why

  • Your email address: to create your account, to sign you in by emailing you a one-time link, to send the other emails you ask for (a change of address, a deleted account), and to identify you on the polls you create, are invited to or vote in. It is never shown to other users. The creator of a poll you were invited to, and CrowdPoll's administrators, can see it.
  • Your Google, Apple, Microsoft or Amazon account: if you log in with one of them, it tells us the name and email address you choose to share, and we use them as above. If you log in with Google, we keep a copy of the profile picture it shares, and show it to you where your avatar appears. We keep no contacts or anything else from your account there, and no picture from any other provider. The copy is kept on our content delivery network at an address that cannot be guessed and that we give only to you, though anyone holding that address could load it. If you delete your account it is removed within minutes, a cached copy can last up to a day, and the network may hold cached copies outside Canada. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we store the relay address Apple gives us as your email address.
  • Your user name and avatar: shown with your polls and, on polls that do not hide voters, with your votes.
  • Your polls and votes: the polls you create, and the choices you make on polls, with the time. When you add a choice to a poll that does not hide voters, the poll keeps which account added it; only the poll's creator sees it, and it is removed when your account is deleted.
  • Addresses other people give us: a poll's creator may type your address to invite you to a private poll, or to bar you from one. We use it only for that poll. If you have no account and want it removed, write to us.
  • Your IP address: used for a few minutes to limit how many emails or requests one connection can make, which stops abuse. It is not stored with your account or your session.
  • Mail you send us: kept to answer you.

Apart from the name a login provider shares, we do not ask for, and do not want, your name, age, location, payment details or anything else.

We send no poll update emails. If we offer them, they will go only to people who opt in.

Invitations to a private poll: when a poll's creator invites you to an Invitation Only poll, we email you the invitation at their request, and at most one reminder if they re-send it. We use your address only for that poll, unless you accept: accepting gives you an ordinary CrowdPoll account, the same as anyone else's and not limited to that poll. The invitation's button can sign you in to CrowdPoll once, for up to 30 days or until the poll ends; after that, or once it has been used, it signs nobody in. If the creator removes you after you were emailed, the poll keeps your address, in a normalized form, only so that adding you back does not email you again; it goes when the poll is deleted. If you have no account and want your address removed from a poll, write to privacy@crowdpoll.vote; it is removed with the poll in any case.

Bot and AI Agent Detection

Before a login starts, on the Login, Create Account and invitation pages, you tick a box that checks you are not a bot or an AI agent.

  • What it measures: characteristics of your device and browser (such as how it draws graphics, plays audio and measures fonts), the timing of your pointer, touch and typing on the page, and details of your connection.
  • Why: to prevent bots and AI agents from creating accounts, sending login emails, and voting on polls.
  • Where: on CrowdPoll's own servers, using the open-source FCaptcha software. It is never sent to or shared with a third party.
  • How long: held in memory for at most one hour, then gone. It is not stored with your account. This is separate from the device evidence described below.
  • Cookies: the check sets no cookie and stores nothing in your browser.

Device Evidence

  • What it records: when you log in and each time you vote, your account, the time, which poll for a vote, and two scrambled codes made from characteristics of your browser and device (such as how it draws graphics, and your screen, processor count, time zone and platform). It never records how you voted.
  • Why: CrowdPoll's rule is one account per person. These records let us look for one device voting from several accounts. A shared device is normal, so a match alone does not mean anyone broke the rule. It is never used for advertising.
  • Where: on CrowdPoll's own servers, using the open-source FingerprintJS software. It is never sent to or shared with a third party. The codes are scrambled with a secret key, so they mean nothing outside CrowdPoll, and the characteristics themselves are not kept.
  • Who sees it: only CrowdPoll's operators. Poll creators and other users cannot.
  • How long: 3 months, removed once a month, so about 4 months at most. It is deleted at once with your account, and a vote's record is deleted with its poll.
  • Cookies: it keeps no cookie and stores nothing in your browser. The software checks whether your browser accepts cookies by setting a test cookie and removing it at once.

9. Your rights

  • Delete your account: This deletes your account, your sessions, your bookmarks and the polls you created with their votes. Your votes on other people's polls stay counted but no longer carry your address or name; they show as "Deleted user". If you were barred from a poll, that record stays, so deleting an account cannot be used to get around the ban. If you changed your email address, anything recorded under your earlier address can be removed by request at privacy@crowdpoll.vote.
  • See your data: write to privacy@crowdpoll.vote and we will send you a copy within 30 days.
  • Correct your data: change your user name or email address on your Profile page, or write to us.
  • Complain: if you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or to the data protection authority where you live.
